## 📘 **Overview**

**The Avengers** is a 2012 American superhero film produced by *Marvel Studios* and distributed by *Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures*.  
It’s the **sixth film** in the *Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)* and the **first crossover** to unite Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye.

## 🌟 **Main Characters**

- 🧠 **Iron Man (Tony Stark)** – Genius billionaire with advanced armor.  
- 🛡️ **Captain America (Steve Rogers)** – Super soldier and natural leader.  
- ⚡ **Thor** – The God of Thunder from Asgard.  
- 💪 **Hulk (Bruce Banner)** – Scientist who turns into a green powerhouse.  
- 🕵️ **Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)** – Expert spy and martial artist.  
- 🏹 **Hawkeye (Clint Barton)** – Sharp-eyed master archer.
